The Science Section

Small label:
THE NEUROSCIENCE

Headline:
Not another to-do list. A schedule built on how your brain actually works.

Card 1:
Title: Chronotype
Body: Your biological clock determines when your brain reaches peak cognitive performance. For some students that is 8am. For others it is 8pm. Forcing the wrong schedule onto the wrong chronotype doesn't build discipline — it destroys output. Pragma identifies your type and places your hardest work at your biological prime time.
Source: Roenneberg et al, Munich Chronotype Questionnaire

Card 2:
Title: Ultradian Rhythm
Body: The human brain naturally cycles through approximately 90-minute windows of high focus followed by rest. Working beyond this window without recovery doesn't produce more — it degrades quality and accelerates fatigue. Pragma sizes your study blocks to match these natural cycles rather than fighting them.
Source: Peretz Lavie, Sleep Research Institute

Card 3:
Title: Motivation Architecture
Body: Self-Determination Theory establishes that people differ fundamentally in how deadline proximity affects their performance. Some students produce their best work close to a deadline. Others perform best when they start early. Pragma identifies which type you are and schedules your tasks accordingly — not generically.
Source: Deci and Ryan, University of Rochester, 1985–2026
